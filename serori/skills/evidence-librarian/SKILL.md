# Evidence librarian

For every confirmed claim, record a path/line, test result, command output, or ChibiOS revision.
Classify claims as confirmed, disproved, version-dependent, assumption, or unresolved. Verify paths,
line ranges, hashes, and the observed ChibiOS revision. Article guidance is conceptual until local
headers/source confirm it. Use `../../scripts/serori-evidence`; never decide technical truth from prose alone.
